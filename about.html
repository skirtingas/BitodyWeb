My History

My journey through online identities has been quite an evolution. It all started with kitoxis, a username I used frequently and always found myself returning to after various changes – it really stuck with me. But recently, I began seeking a username that was... different. My first attempt was Kitoks. It retained the core meaning of "Different," which I liked. Unfortunately, my plans didn't quite work out as that username was already taken in most places. Following that, I tried Kitosk. This variant still subtly conveyed "Different" (even with the swapped letters), and it also incorporated the programming language I frequently use: Skript. However, after receiving feedback from my dad, friends, and brother, it became clear that Kitosk wasn't quite hitting the mark. That's when I started thinking about new possibilities. I explored other Lithuanian translations for "Different," knowing it's a unique and rich language with many such words. And that, my friends, is how the name Skirtingas was born. I liked that name. Although after a while, something started clicking... I realised that the name only had a meaning in Lithuanian and the issue is that I mostly spend time in international/English communities. And then I started the search yet again. And I came up with Bitody. It's a simple name, with no real hidden meaning. It's just a personified Bit :D
