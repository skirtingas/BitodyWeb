About Me

Who am I?

I am Bitody—or as some might know me, Skirtingas. I consider myself a chill developer, and I'm also a musician, specifically a pianist.

I've been playing the piano for over 8 years, though I only started spending serious time and energy on it about 2 years ago.

I'm also a big Minecraft fan and player. I enjoy PvP, but what I mostly do in Minecraft is create custom experiences for others.

I make things other people can either use or enjoy, primarily Minecraft Plugins and Servers. Currently, a project called Playward is on hold because I'm focusing on another project. I don't want to spoil too much about Playward!

Right now, as I'm building this website, I'm focused on learning web development.

My History

My journey through online names has been quite a trip.

It all started with kitoxis, a name I used often and always found myself returning to after various changes—it really stuck with me.

But recently, I started looking for a name that was... different.

My first try was Kitoks. It kept the core meaning of "Different," which I liked. Sadly, my plan didn't quite work out because that name was already taken in most places.

Next, I tried Kitosk. This variant still subtly meant "Different" (even with the swapped letters), and it also brought in the programming language I use a lot: Skript.

However, after getting feedback from my dad, friends, and brother, it was clear that Kitosk wasn't quite right.

That's when I started thinking about new ideas. I looked for other Lithuanian words for "Different," since it's a unique and expressive language. And that, my friends, is how the name Skirtingas was created.

I liked that name, but after a while, something clicked. I realized the name only had meaning in Lithuanian, and the problem is that I mostly spend time in international/English communities.

So I started the search again and came up with Bitody. It's a simple name, with no real hidden meaning. It's just a personified Bit :D

Don't worry, I'm not giving up on being Skirtingas—that will simply be the name of my second account, which I'll mostly use for Lithuanian projects.

History of Starfal

Before it became Starfal, my project had a different name: RedSRV.

Originally, Starfal wasn't even meant to be a developer team; it started its life as a Minecraft server focused on the BoxPVP game mode.

Unfortunately, after separating from the other "Owner," my desire to keep the BoxPVP server going faded. That was the point where I switched my focus to plugin development instead.

After about a year of only maintaining Starfal's plugins and offering support, I realized something: I was the only active member within what was supposedly the "Developer Team."

Because of this, I decided to officially move the team's assets and projects under my sole control, as that's effectively how they had been operating anyway.
