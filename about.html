Who am I?

I am Bitody... Or as some might know me - Skirtingas. I am, as I would say, a Chill Developer. I'm also a musician, in particular - a pianist.
I have been playing the piano for over 8 years, although I only started giving it more time and energy around 2 years ago.
I'm also a very big Minecraft fan and player. I like doing PvP, but what I mostly do in Minecraft is make experiences.
I make things other people can either use or enjoy. I make Minecraft Plugins and Servers. Currently, a project called Playward, which I don't want to spoil much about, is on hold, because of another project.
Right now, as I'm making this website, I'm learning web development.

My History

My journey through online identities has been quite an evolution.
It all started with kitoxis, a username I used frequently and always found myself returning to after various changes – it really stuck with me.
But recently, I began seeking a username that was... different.
My first attempt was Kitoks. It retained the core meaning of "Different," which I liked. Unfortunately, my plans didn't quite work out as that username was already taken in most places.
Following that, I tried Kitosk. This variant still subtly conveyed "Different" (even with the swapped letters), and it also incorporated the programming language I frequently use: Skript.
However, after receiving feedback from my dad, friends, and brother, it became clear that Kitosk wasn't quite hitting the mark.
That's when I started thinking about new possibilities. I explored other Lithuanian translations for "Different," knowing it's a unique and rich language with many such words. And that, my friends, is how the name Skirtingas was born.
I liked that name. Although after a while, something started clicking... I realised that the name only had a meaning in Lithuanian and the issue is that I mostly spend time in international/English communities.
And then I started the search yet again. And I came up with Bitody. It's a simple name, with no real hidden meaning. It's just a personified Bit :D
Don't worry, I'm not giving up on being Skirtingas - That will just be the name of my second account, which I will mostly use in Lithuanian projects.

History of Starfal

Before it became Starfal, my project had a different name: RedSRV.
Originally, Starfal wasn't even conceived as a developer team; it began its life as a Minecraft server, specifically focused on the BoxPVP game mode.
Unfortunately, after cutting ties with the other "Owner," my enthusiasm for continuing the BoxPVP server waned. It was at that point that I shifted my focus towards plugin development instead.
After approximately a year of solely maintaining Starfal's plugins and providing support, I came to a realization: I was the only active member within what was nominally the "Developer Team."
Given this, I decided to formally transition the team's assets and projects into my own, consolidating them under my sole direction, as they had effectively been operating that way already.
